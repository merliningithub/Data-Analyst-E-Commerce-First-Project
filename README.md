# Data-Analyst-E-Commerce-First-Project

An end-to-end e-commerce sales analysis covering sales performance, customer behaviour, product trends, regional sales and returns, with an interactive Power BI dashboard.

**Tools:** Python (Pandas), MySQL, Power BI, Google Sheets

---

## Dashboard Preview

![E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard](page%201%20.png)

---

## Business Problem

An e-commerce business needs a clear view of which products, countries and customers drive revenue, and where returns are hurting performance. This project turns raw transaction data into insights that help stakeholders decide where to focus sales, inventory and quality efforts.

**Key questions**
- How much revenue, how many orders, units and customers does the business have?
- Which countries generate the most sales?
- Which products lead in revenue and in quantity sold?
- Which customers contribute the most to sales?
- How significant are product returns?

---

## Approach

1. **Data cleaning and transformation (Python, Pandas):** handled missing values, duplicates and data types, and created derived fields such as total sales.
2. **Data analysis (MySQL):** wrote SQL queries to calculate total sales, average sales, top products, regional sales, returns, orders and customers.
3. **Dashboard (Power BI):** built an interactive dashboard with KPI cards and visuals to monitor performance in one view.
4. **Insights and recommendations:** turned the findings into actions for stakeholders.

## Key Insights

| Area | Insight |
|---|---|
| Revenue market | The **United Kingdom** is the dominant market, far ahead of EIRE, Germany and the Netherlands |
| Products | A small group of top products drives a large share of sales and units |
| Customers | Sales are concentrated among a limited number of customers |
| Returns | Returned quantity is significant enough to investigate |

---

## Recommendations

- **Prioritise top-performing products** in inventory planning and promotions.
- **Strengthen sales strategies in high-performing regions** such as the United Kingdom, and explore growth in smaller markets.
- **Investigate product returns** to find root causes and reduce revenue loss.
- **Retain high-value customers** through loyalty and targeted offers.
